## Governing principle

Track A determines the journal-primary architecture from the complete frozen comparative evidence. The preprint architecture has no privileged status.

## Track B

May proceed before Track-A model selection:
- prediction-blind provenance/independence audit;
- source-family tracing and candidate-byte hashing;
- ontology/support mapping;
- candidate-specific sealing that does not expose model predictions.

Must wait until Track-A selection is sealed:
- classifier inference on the external cohort;
- any model-specific external comparison.

Track-B outcomes may not choose or tune the Track-A model.

## Track C

May proceed before Track-A model selection:
- generic runtime/export infrastructure QA;
- device/toolchain qualification;
- preservation and analysis of historical MobileNetV4 PTE evidence.

Must wait until Track-A selection is sealed:
- journal-primary model export/quantization;
- model-specific journal runtime/device claims.

The selected Track-A architecture is the default Track-C journal candidate. If it fails a separately frozen deployment requirement, report the scientific-primary and deployment-primary models separately; do not rewrite the Track-A winner.

## Protected V1

`DS-V1-TEST-CONSUMED` remains closed through the strengthening amendment, model selection, and pre-handoff audit unless a later separately authorized continuity gate explicitly opens it.

## Manuscript synthesis

The final paper must distinguish:
- historical preprint architecture/result;
- Track-A scientific-primary architecture;
- Track-B external-validity evidence;
- Track-C deployment-primary result if it differs from the scientific-primary architecture.
