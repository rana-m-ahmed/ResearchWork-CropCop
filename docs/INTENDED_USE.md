# Intended use and safety

CropCop is a research system for closed-set plant-health image recognition, benchmark forensics, long-tail analysis, compression, and runtime-fidelity studies.

It is not a substitute for expert agronomic diagnosis, cannot identify unsupported conditions, and may be overconfident under domain shift. Consequential use requires source-independent validation, calibrated abstention, user-facing uncertainty, expert escalation, and monitoring for acquisition drift.
